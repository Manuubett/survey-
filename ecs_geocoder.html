ℹ️ How this works This tool geocodes every ECS-2026 polling station using the free OpenStreetMap Nominatim API (1 request/second to respect rate limits). Stations successfully found get real GPS coordinates. Stations not found fall back to a small deterministic offset around their ward centre — always inside Embu County bounds, never in the dam. When done, download ecs_ps_coords.json and ecs_polling_stations_geo.js and drop them into your survey folder.
